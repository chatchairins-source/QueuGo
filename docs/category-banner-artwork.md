# Customer category banner assets

Final owner instruction: ordinary everyday Thai shops and approachable normal vendors. Mobile: glass-counter phone kiosk in a mall. Computer: small local sales/repair shop. Automotive: roadside parts shop. Fashion/toys: ordinary local stalls. Home: basic household goods shop. Laundry: simple clean local washing shop. Shopping: modest neighborhood general shop. Practical shelves and materials, normal daylight/fluorescent lighting, no luxury/designer showroom styling.

Everyone wears a BLACK COLLARED BUTTON-DOWN SHIRT with ONE RED Q emblem on the chest, no apron or white/light shirt. Built-in imagegen creates natural realistic wide category photographs filled with bright shop/merchandise detail across the frame. No dark left panel/strip, no empty text zone, no vignette/gradient/overlay baked into the photo, no embedded text/prices/watermarks/manufacturer logos.

Final files: `assets/banners/qg_<category>_banner.webp`; Android mirrors: `native-android/customer/src/main/res/drawable-nodpi/`. WebP960x458 <=50KiB, byte-identical copies. Existing UI uses dark text over a soft white translucent wash. Images belong in banners; category icon buttons stay unchanged. Category artworks never replace actual store/product photos or create business data.
